 A)🎯 Game Objective
 The goal is simple but challenging:
 Score the highest points by shooting the white puck into your opponent's goal before the 90-second timer runs out.
 But be careful, the bot opponent watches your every move and won't forgive your mistakes!

 B)📜 Basic Rules and Mechanics
 1. Race Against Time: Matches last exactly 90 seconds. There is no first half or break!
 2. You must maintain your stamina and concentration until the last second of the match.

 C) Trampoline Physics: The biggest feature that makes Rush Flicker special is its Spring-Bounce (Trampoline) physics.
 You don't just hit the puck; you launch it like a spring!
 The angle and speed at which you hit the puck makes it bounce around the field like a bullet.

 D) Dynamic Friction: The puck slides as if on ice but slows down very slightly over time.
 This prevents the game from turning into uncontrolled chaos and gives you the opportunity to strategize.

 E) 🎮 Controls (How to Play)
 Movement: Control your hockey player using the Arrow Keys on your keyboard.

 F) Offense: Hit the puck hard to shoot it toward the opponent's goal.
 Remember, the harder you hit it, the stronger the trampoline effect!

 G) Defense: Stand between the puck and the goal to protect your goal. You can gently touch the puck to slow it down.

 H)💡 Pro Tips
 Use the Walls: Instead of shooting directly, you can bounce the puck off the side walls to catch your opponent off guard.

 I) Don't Panic: If the ball is coming at you too fast, instead of diving at it, stand still in front of the goal and wait for the puck to hit you and bounce back.

 J) Speed Control: Instead of constantly moving fast, position yourself to intercept the puck at the right moment.
 In this game, timing is just as important as speed.
